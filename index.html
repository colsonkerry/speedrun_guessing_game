How to play

You'll see two games. Pick the one you think has the faster speedrun world record.

After you guess, both times are revealed. A run is 5 rounds, and the squares in the middle show how you did: green for correct, red for incorrect.

Each time is the current world record in that game's most popular category, which is shown under its title.

Why are the game images getting cropped?

Try zooming out on the page or playing on a different resolution.

If you are still having troubles, please report an issue on the Github page. Thank you!
